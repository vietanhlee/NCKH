\textbf{Specific subject area} & Intelligent Transportation Systems, Computer Vision, Spatio-Temporal Data Mining, Self-Supervised Learning \\
\midrule
\textbf{Type of data} & Image time-series (JPEG snapshots), Geographic metadata (CSV), Directed spatial routing distance matrices (NumPy / CSV / XLSX), Python dataset loaders and graph utility routines \\
\midrule
\textbf{How data were acquired} & Automated continuous retrieval from the public traffic surveillance portal of Ho Chi Minh City (\url{https://giaothong.hochiminhcity.gov.vn}) via an asynchronous HTTP polling engine. Road network routing distances were computed via the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) engine using OpenStreetMap road network data. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Data format} & Raw (JPEG snapshots, $512 \times 288$ px), Filtered (municipal transmission placeholder cards excised), Structured (CSV metadata, NumPy matrices) \\
\midrule
\textbf{Parameters for data collection} & Multi-camera time-lapse snapshot polling at approximately $\Delta T \approx 300$~s (5 minutes, $\sim 12$~snapshots/hour/station) across 7 continuous days (168 consecutive hours, Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59 ICT). Average client crawl time lag relative to camera on-screen clock: $\Delta t_{\text{lag}} = 15.0 \pm 4.2$~s. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Description of data collection} & Over 1,000,000 discrete surveillance snapshots ($\approx 1.22$ million total frames) collected from 608 active municipal camera stations mounted at $6.0$--$15.0$~m elevation across Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Raw snapshots undergo automated integrity checking to filter out $284 \times 177$ error placeholder cards. Directed road distances were computed for camera node pairs within a 5.0~km spatial cutoff horizon, yielding 2,420 valid directed corridors. \\
