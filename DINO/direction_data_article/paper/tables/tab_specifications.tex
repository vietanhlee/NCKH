\textbf{Type of data} & Image time-series (JPEG snapshots), Geographic and camera metadata (CSV: \texttt{camera\_data\_608Cam.csv}, \texttt{routes.csv}), Directed spatial routing distance matrices (NumPy / CSV / XLSX), Python dataset loaders and graph utility routines \\
\midrule
\textbf{How data were acquired} & Automated continuous retrieval from the public traffic surveillance portal of Ho Chi Minh City (\url{https://giaothong.hochiminhcity.gov.vn}) via an asynchronous HTTP polling engine. Road network routing distances were computed via the Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) engine using OpenStreetMap road network data. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Data format} & Raw (JPEG snapshots, $512 \times 288$ px), Filtered (municipal transmission placeholder cards excised), Structured (CSV metadata, NumPy matrices) \\
\midrule
\textbf{Parameters for data collection} & Multi-camera time-lapse snapshot polling at an empirical interval of $\Delta T = 269.0 \pm 239.7$~s (median: $263.0$~s, nominal: 5.0 minutes) across 93.6 continuous hours (3.9 calendar days, from 02/10/2026 17:37 to 06/10/2026 15:12 ICT). Total uncompressed dataset volume: 44.38~GB. Client crawl latency: $\Delta t_{\text{lag}} = 15.0 \pm 4.2$~s. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Description of data collection} & A total of 714,123 surveillance snapshots collected from 608 indexed municipal camera stations across Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (572 continuous active stations yielding 1,250--1,268 frames each; 36 stations with initial stream connection dropouts). High-angle mast mounting at $6.0$--$15.0$~m elevation. Raw snapshots undergo automated integrity checking to filter out $284 \times 177$ error placeholder cards. Directed road distances were computed for camera node pairs within a 6.0~km spatial cutoff horizon, yielding 2,450 valid directed corridors. \\
